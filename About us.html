SCHOOL HISTORY

Dawn bells nursery and primary school is a mixed day and boarding primary school that was founded to help struggling parents provide quality education to their children at affordable prices. The founder, Ms. Nabimanya Annah after observing that children in rural areas like any child deserved good education services,started the school in 2012 in Bwizibwera-rutooma town council . The school was started to provide holistic education to the children.
